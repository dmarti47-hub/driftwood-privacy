Driftwood Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Driftwood is a nature soundscape app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Driftwood does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Driftwood has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your use of the app to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Driftwood saves a few settings on your phone so it can remember your preferences: your saved mixes and volume levels, the last place you opened, and whether you have unlocked the full set of places. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. Driftwood never sees your payment details. Google handles your purchase under the Google Privacy Policy; Driftwood only receives confirmation that the unlock is owned so it can open the extra places.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show playback controls on your lock screen while sounds are playing.
  • Background playback and keeping the device awake — so sounds keep playing with the screen locked until you stop them.
  • Internet — used only to talk to Google Play for purchases. All sounds and images are stored in the app and play offline.

Links to other websites

The About page links to the original sources and licences of the recordings and photos. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Driftwood is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
